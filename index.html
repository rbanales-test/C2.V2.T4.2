Wavetable Synthesis Simulator

Objective: Understand how wavetable scanning works by navigating through a "flipbook" of waveform frames to create evolving digital textures.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator.
  2. This is a 100% guided exercise. Follow the 5 sequential missions exactly as instructed.
  3. Instead of a static waveform, a Wavetable is a sequence of frames. Moving through them is called scanning.
  4. You can use the large button or your Spacebar / Enter key to trigger the sound.
